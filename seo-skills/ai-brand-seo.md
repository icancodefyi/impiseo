# AI Era SEO: Brand, Discovery & Action-Driven Search

## Three Key Shifts

### 1. AI Assistants Cite Diverse Sources
- 86% of top cited sources are unique to each AI assistant
- Google AI Overviews: leans on YouTube, Reddit, Quora
- ChatGPT: prefers publishers and news outlets
- Perplexity: tends to cite niche and regional sites
- Getting mentioned on these domains won't automatically put you in AI overviews — every niche is different

**Action:** Use a brand radar tool (e.g., H/S Brand Radar) to reverse-engineer which sites AI cites in your niche. Enter your brand + competitors → open "Cited Domains" report.

### 2. Brand is Everything
- When you ask an AI a query, it fans out into dozens of subqueries (called "query phantom")
- Example: "best smartphone for photography" → subqueries like "iPhone 17 Pro vs Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra camera", "best phone for low-light photos", "smartphone with best zoom lens 2026"
- AI pulls from pages that rank for each subquery and stitches them together
- If your brand is mentioned across those ranking pages, you're far more likely to be included
- Branded mentions have the strongest correlation with AI overview visibility — more than backlinks, referring domains, and domain rating

**Action:** Use brand radar → "Cited Pages" report → filter queries containing: best, top, versus, review, alternative. These are predictable fan-out queries. Get your brand mentioned on list roundups, comparison posts, and dedicated reviews.

### 3. Organic Clicks Flow to Action-Driven Queries
- AI is great at answering informational queries (what is, how to)
- AI cannot satisfy action-driven queries (calculator, checker, generator, service, buy)
- Google knows user needs to click or transact → no AI overview for those

**Action:** Before targeting a query, ask: "Can AI fully satisfy the user for this query?" If no, traditional SEO (content, links, technical) still works.

**Find action queries:**
- Filter keywords with modifiers: calculator, checker, generator, tool, service, near me, price
- Filter by transactional intent in keyword research tools

## Summary
- SEO hasn't ended — it split into three buckets
- Be discoverable everywhere (AI sources)
- Build a brand that gets mentioned on lists, comparisons, reviews, social media
- Traditional SEO still wins for action-driven queries
- Adapt to search as searcher behavior changes
